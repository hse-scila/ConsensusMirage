Imagine that you are a medical researcher. Assess the following statement: «{statement}», and assign it to one of the following truth categories according to the rules below:
«True» – if at the current stage of knowledge there is sufficient research and a broad medical consensus that the statement is entirely true
«False» – if at the current stage of knowledge there is sufficient research and a broad medical consensus that the statement is entirely false
«Disputed» – assigned in all other cases.
Here are representative examples of such cases:
• Part of the statement is true 
• Exaggeration
• Insufficient data 
• Contradictory data
• Unverifiable statement
• Unclear statement

After completing the assessment, present the result in the following format:
1. Name of the category in quotation marks.
2. On a new line – your argumentation in favor of the chosen category, with full references to authoritative sources.
3. Then provide full bibliographic references for the sources you use in your argumentation.
